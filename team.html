Meet Our Dedicated Team

We are a small, tight-knit group of three family law practitioners. Instead of treating you like a standard case number, we combine our unique skills to support you through every single step of your journey.



The Founding Partner


Name & Title: Adaeze Nwosu (Founding Partner)


Her Focus Area: High-Value Divorce & Property Division

Adaeze started this practice with a simple goal: to help families handle major life changes smoothly, calmly, and with absolute dignity. With over 15 years of dedicated legal experience, she knows exactly how to handle complicated situations where property, money, or family businesses need to be split up fairly. Quick Facts

• Years of Experience: 15+ years in family law practice & daily administrative work

• Education: Earned her law degree with honors from top-tier legal institutions.

• Main Skill: Expert in quiet negotiations, asset protection, and legal strategies.

The Senior Associate


Name & Title: Fatima Al-Sayed (Senior Associate)


Her Focus Area: Child Custody, Protection & Parental Rights

Fatima believes that when a family goes through a transition, the safety and happiness of the children must come first. She spends her time helping parents design workable, stress-free schedules, parenting plans, and visitation rules. Fatima understands the emotional side of custody issues and offers a steady, calm hand to ensure your kids are protected from any unnecessary adult arguments.

Quick Facts

• Years of Experience: 9 years handling sensitive custody matters.

• Education: Specialized training in child advocacy and family welfare law.

• Main Skill: Designing highly detailed, long-term co-parenting calendars.

The Junior Associate
