Our Practice Areas

Legal challenges don't have to be overwhelming. We provide plain-English guidance and steady support to help you make the best decisions for your future.



Divorce & Property Division

Splitting up a life together is complicated. We help you sort through your shared property, bank accounts, investments, and family homes fairly and calmly. Our goal is to protect your financial security and make sure assets are divided properly without dragging the process out or spending all your hard-earned savings on endless legal fights.

Key things we help with

• Filing for legal separation or divorce.

• Dividing houses, land, and shared family property.

• Protecting individual business assets and personal savings.

• Reviewing prenuptial and postnuptial agreements.

Custody, Visitation & Child Support

When parents separate, protecting the kids is always the top priority. We help you build clear, workable co-parenting schedules and visitation plans that keep your children safe, happy, and out of the middle of parental arguments. We also handle child support calculations to ensure your kids have the financial foundation they need for school, healthcare, and daily life.

Key things we help with

• Designing fair, step-by-step parenting schedules and calendars.

• Establishing legal custody and decision-making rights for school and health.

• Calculating or adjusting monthly child support payments.

• Updating custody plans as children grow older and their routines change.

Family Mediation Services

You do not have to go to court to solve a family disagreement. Mediation is a quiet, private process where our trained lawyers act as neutral helpers to guide you and your partner toward a mutual agreement. We sit down together in a calm office meeting room to talk through problems rationally, saving you time, court fees, and the emotional stress of an angry public trial.

Key things we help with

• Hosting private mediation sessions outside of the court system.

• Helping couples write down their own agreed terms for divorce or custody.

• Resolving ongoing family disagreements quietly and respectfully.

• Keeping your family's private matters completely out of public court records.
